About me

I am currently a PhD Candidate at the ETH Zürich and the German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ), while also affiliated with Helmholtz Imaging. I am interested in enabling causality statements about (either?) model or data. The focus of my research lies in explanatory AI (XAI) and probabilistic modelling with application in computer vision. Especially, I am working with attribution methods, interpretable representations, uncertainty explanations, and AI as a statistical method in medical imaging.
