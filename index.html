Welcome to my Portfolio.

My name is Lukas and I am currently studying statistics at the ETH Zürich and the University of Geneva. Welcome to my portfolio page! Over the course of my studies, professional, and private programming life I completed a selection of projects where I want to share some of them on this website. They are all located in the areas of classical Statistics, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence and programmed in Python, R or Julia. Some projects can extend this scope, for example a web application via the Shiny framework in R. But have a look for yourself:

Statistics

Tumour Size Growth Analysis

Estimation and Inference of Generalized Linear Mixed Effects Models (GLMM) to proof the effectiveness of a tumour growth rate reducing medicine applied to mice.

Features influencing Language Acquesition

Determining the factors behind the ability of humans to be aware of language and to understand it based on the Generalized Equation Estimation (GEE). Includes exploratory data analysis, model validation and robust estimation.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Model Selection to estimate Online Ads Quality

 

Comparison of a broad spectrum of Machine- and Deep Learning Models. Includes feature engineering, hyperparameter optimization and inference per model.

Making predictions based on corrupted MRI Data

Predicting the age of patients based on low quality magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) data. Main focus was on outlier removal via dimension reduction with Support Vector Classifiers (SVC) and feature selection by regression- and LASSO-based methods.

The Effect of Sparse Coding visualized in Application

Comparison of Principal Component Analysis (PCA) with non-negative matrix factorization (NNMF) using an overcomplete dictionary. Different levels of sparsity in the activation matrix of the NNMF algorithm show, that sparse coding can increase performance for denoising and compression of pictures.
