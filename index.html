About me

I am a PostDoc at EPFL and the University Hospital Zürich, working with Charlotte Bunne and Andreas Wicki.

My interdisciplinary research advances personalized medicine by developing AI for spatial omics technologies that tackle diagnostic, therapeutic, and discovery tasks.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at ETH Zürich and the German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ), supervised by Joachim Buhmann and Paul Jäger.

🎓 Academic Education

  • [PostDoc] Joint PostDoc in the AIMM Group at EPFL and the Department of Oncology at the University Hospital Zürich
  • [Ph.D.] Computer Science Ph.D. in the ISE Group at ETH Zürich and the IML Group at the DKFZ
  • [Ph.D.] DKFZ Fellowship recipient and completed the Helmholtz International Graduate School for Cancer Research
  • [Ph.D.] External member of the IVIA Lab at ETH Zürich
  • Master Thesis & Research Intern at ETH Zürich
  • M.Sc. in Statistics at University of Geneva
  • Intern at Microsoft
